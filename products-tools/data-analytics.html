IBM Synthetic Data Sets

AI Lifecycle: Prepare Data

What are IBM Synthetic Data Sets?

IBM Synthetic Data Sets are enterprise-grade, artificially generated datasets designed for AI model development in financial services. They look and behave like real transaction data, without containing any real customer information, making them safe to use for building, testing, and demonstrating AI models without requiring access to production data.

One of the most common blockers in early-stage AI projects is data access. Obtaining approved access to production data can take time, and in regulated industries there may be restrictions that make data difficult to use for development. IBM Synthetic Data Sets help remove that blocker, allowing teams to move from idea to working model without waiting on data approvals.

Why Use It?

  • Removes the data access blocker — No need to request, anonymize, or wait for production data. Start building immediately.
  • Realistic financial services data — Datasets reflect the statistical properties and patterns of real financial transactions, not random noise.
  • No seed data required — IBM generates the data entirely. Organizations do not need to provide any real data to get started.
  • Privacy and compliance friendly — No real customer data means no privacy concerns during development, testing, demonstrations, and proof-of-concept activities.
  • Supports multiple lifecycle stages — Use the same datasets to build models, validate results, and demonstrate AI solutions.

Common LinuxONE Use Cases

IBM Synthetic Data Sets are frequently used to support:

  • Machine learning experimentation
  • Generative AI proof of concepts
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) demonstrations
  • Fraud detection development
  • AI model validation and testing
  • OpenShift AI workshops and training environments

By providing realistic data without exposing sensitive information, teams can accelerate AI development while maintaining security and compliance requirements.
