Why Organizations Use Generative AI

Generative AI helps organizations unlock value from large volumes of information by making knowledge easier to access, understand, and use. Instead of requiring users to search through documents, applications, or data sources manually, generative AI can analyze information and generate summaries, answers, recommendations, and other useful outputs.

By leveraging foundation models and large language models (LLMs), organizations can support a wide range of tasks without building a separate model for every use case.

Common Benefits

Unlock Value from Enterprise Knowledge
Unlock value from enterprise knowledge at scale by enabling users to interact with large volumes of structured and unstructured information through natural language instead of manually searching documents, databases, or applications. Foundation models can be adapted to support many tasks using the same underlying model, reducing the need to build separate AI solutions for each use case.
Improve Productivity and Decision-Making
Improve productivity and accelerate decision-making through capabilities such as summarization, conversational search, question answering, and content generation. By reducing the time spent gathering, reviewing, and synthesizing information, users can focus on higher-value business activities.
Reduce AI Adoption Cost and Complexity
Reduce the cost and complexity of AI adoption by leveraging pre-trained foundation models that require significantly less labeled data and shorter training cycles than traditional approaches. Organizations can adapt existing models to new use cases rather than developing models from scratch.
Extend AI Across Business Functions
Extend AI across a broad range of business functions including customer service, business automation, IT operations, application modernization, knowledge management, and software development. A single foundation model can support multiple downstream tasks and workflows.

Common Use Cases

Conversational Search & Q&A
Enable users to ask questions in natural language and receive responses derived from enterprise knowledge, policies, procedures, and business information.
Document Summarization
Condense large reports, meeting notes, technical documentation, and business content into concise summaries that accelerate information consumption and decision-making.
Content Generation
Generate business content, communications, documentation, and knowledge assets while improving consistency and reducing manual effort.
Code Generation and Application Modernization
Assist developers by generating, explaining, and transforming code, helping accelerate software delivery and modernization initiatives.
Enterprise Assistants
Provide contextual assistance for business and technical users by combining foundation models with enterprise knowledge, applications, and workflows.
