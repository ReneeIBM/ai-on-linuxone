Why Organizations Use Agentic AI

While generative AI helps users create content and obtain information, agentic AI focuses on accomplishing tasks and achieving outcomes. Agentic systems use AI agents to reason through requests, determine the steps required to complete them, interact with tools and data sources, and execute actions across business processes and IT environments.

Organizations are adopting agentic AI to move beyond AI-assisted work toward greater automation of repetitive, complex, and multi-step activities.

Common Benefits

From Insights to Actions
Move beyond AI-generated insights to AI-driven actions. Agentic AI enables systems to reason about goals, determine the actions required to achieve them, and execute tasks across tools, applications, and workflows with limited supervision.
Automate Complex Multi-Step Processes
Automate complex multi-step processes that would traditionally require manual coordination across people, systems, and business functions. Agents can plan actions, select tools, execute tasks, and iterate toward an objective.
Increase Operational Efficiency
Increase operational efficiency and productivity by reducing repetitive work and enabling AI systems to assist with execution, not just recommendations. Organizations can streamline business operations, software delivery, and IT management activities.
Improve Workflow Outcomes
Improve workflow outcomes through reasoning and orchestration. Agentic systems can leverage multiple models, tools, data sources, and specialized agents to solve problems that exceed the capabilities of a single model or application.

Common Use Cases

Business Process Automation
Automate operational workflows such as claims processing, payment processing, fraud investigation, and compliance activities by coordinating actions across multiple systems and data sources.
IT Operations Automation
Optimize infrastructure management, resource utilization, incident response, and operational workflows through intelligent automation and decision support.
AI-Powered Software Delivery
Accelerate development and testing activities through AI-assisted coding, validation, workflow execution, and automated engineering tasks.
Fraud and Compliance Workflows
Assist with activities such as fraud investigations, anti-money laundering reviews, and suspicious activity reporting by gathering information from multiple systems and initiating follow-up actions when risk indicators are detected.
Multi-Step Operational Tasks
Coordinate actions across applications, data sources, business processes, and other agents to complete complex objectives that require planning, tool usage, collaboration, and iterative execution.
