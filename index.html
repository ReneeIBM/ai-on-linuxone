What is AI on IBM Z (IBM Z AI)?

AI on IBM Z covers AI implementations for business use cases where data originates and lives on IBM Z and where transactions are processed on IBM Z — achieving real-time insights at the point of impact through data colocation and minimal latency. This can involve predictive AI approaches, as well as more advanced approaches using LLMs for generative AI, or the implementation of AI agents. For AI operations and IT productivity tooling (e.g. IBM Assistant for Z), see the teams that own those solutions.

AI on IBM Z features state-of-the-art hardware and software capabilities designed to optimize AI at scale alongside your enterprise's most critical workloads and data. This includes hardware accelerators such as the IBM Telum on-chip inference accelerator, a broad portfolio of IBM software, open-source projects, and an ecosystem of ISV solutions for specific enterprise use cases.

This site is designed to help AI practitioners and architects get started on their journey to leveraging AI in their IBM Z environment — with guidance on the AI lifecycle, products and tools, and example use cases and solution blueprints.

What AI on IBM Z can do for your business

Bring AI closer to your enterprise data

Access and analyze data where it already resides, reducing complexity and accelerating time to insight through colocation of AI and transactions.

Power real-time transactional AI

Embed AI directly into business transactions — from fraud detection and anti-money laundering to personalized offerings — to support faster, more intelligent decision making.

Build on security and resilience

Run AI workloads on the same trusted platform that supports many of the world's most critical business operations.

Simplify the path to production

Reduce architectural complexity and streamline the journey from experimentation to deployment.

Data and AI sovereignty

Keep full control over your data, your AI models, and — for generative AI use cases — even your prompts and inference data. Running AI on-prem on IBM Z means no third-party access to your assets, helping address regulatory and governance requirements.

What you can build on IBM Z

AI on IBM Z supports a wide range of business use cases across all three AI approaches. Below are representative examples of what organizations are running today.

Predictive AI

Fraud detection, anti-money laundering, anomaly detection, client segmentation, churn prediction

Generative AI

Document processing, product suggestions, customer service augmentation, automated report generation

Agentic AI

Suspicious activity report workflows, multi-step fraud response, AI-driven process automation

Advanced analytics

Medical image analysis, geospatial analysis, multi-model AI, unstructured data processing

The AI on IBM Z portfolio includes hardware accelerators, IBM software, open-source frameworks, and ISV solutions. To see how products and capabilities map across the full AI lifecycle, see the AI Lifecycle and Products & Tools sections.
